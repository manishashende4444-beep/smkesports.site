SMK ESPORTS website

1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Keep index.html, style.css, script.js and the assets folder together.
3. Open index.html in a browser, or upload the complete folder contents to your hosting public_html directory.

The APK download buttons point to the SMK ESPORTS APK URL supplied by the user.
The logo image is stored at assets/smk-logo-square.jpg.
